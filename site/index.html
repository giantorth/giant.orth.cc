Your MOZA hardware, with all of SimHub behind it.

AZOM gives SimHub full control of your MOZA gear: every setting Pithouse has, plus SimHub's LED effects, thousands of telemetry properties for the dashboard, and per-game, per-car profiles. One app instead of two.

Feature
sheet

Grouped by what's on your rig. Tabs appear in the plugin only for hardware that's connected.

WheelAll MOZA wheels

  • LEDs in SimHubRPM, flags, limiter and scripted effects through SimHub's effects pipeline.
  • Dashboard telemetryAny SimHub property on any LCD channel, with hot reload.
  • Dashboard uploadManage stored layouts, upload .mzdash, or convert a SimHub dash.
  • Knobs & buttonsPer-knob colours and 200+ bindable actions.

BaseAll MOZA bases

  • Full configurationRotation, FFB, damping, EQ and output curves.
  • LFE hapticsEngine, ABS and gearshift effects at 50 Hz. FW 1.2.10.10+.
  • Ambient ringR16, R21, R25 and R27 light rings as their own SimHub device.

PedalsCRP2 · mBooster · Handbrake

  • Active pedalsmBooster travel in mm and force in kg, with sim input mapping.
  • Passive pedalsCRP2 calibration and output curves, plus handbrake modes.

Pedal hapticsS12 · mBooster

  • S12 vibration unitsOne ShakeIt Motors device per pedal, built in SimHub's own effects editor.
  • mBooster effectsABS, traction control, wheel spin, gear shift, lockup, road texture and custom formulas.

ShiftersAB9 · AB6 · HGP · SGP

  • AB9 / AB6H-patterns and sequential, resistance, spring, damping, vibration, live gate display.
  • HGP + SGPSeparate tabs. Run both on one rig.

StalksMulti-function stalks

  • Any keyboard layoutKeys are stored as scan codes and shown with your layout's own names, so international keyboards can bind any key.
  • Truck-sim modeWipers, lights and indicators sent to ETS2/ATS as rebindable keys, with stage tracking and one-click re-sync.

MotionHMA150

  • SimHub Motion outputGame telemetry and engine vibration streamed to the platform.
  • Platform setupPort setup, self-tests, motion effects, stroke and lighting. Imports Motion Manager presets.

DashCM1 · CM2

  • Default channel mapsTyres, timing, fuel and flags bound out of the box.

SDKEmbedded MOZA SDK server

  • iRacing360 Hz force feedback and LFE, served by AZOM's built-in SDK server.
  • Other gamesDiRT and the RSF build of Richard Burns Rally are supported too.

ProfilesEvery device

  • Per game, per carSettings switch when you launch a title.
  • Pithouse importPresets load with a diff of every changed value.

Install

  1. Download MozaPlugin_<version>.zip from Releases.
  2. Extract MozaPlugin.dll into the SimHub folder.
  3. Start SimHub. AZOM is under Settings › Plugins.
  4. Connect your hardware, restart SimHub, then search Devices for MOZA.
